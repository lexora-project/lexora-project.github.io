Manifesto

Built to reduce friction, not add another layer of it.

Lexora is being shaped as a disciplined language environment rather than a crowded utility stack. The direction is deliberate: faster comprehension, cleaner context, and tooling that stays out of the way when the work matters.

This is not a race to look loud. It is a quiet push toward a sharper reading and translation surface - one that respects focus, precision, and long-session usability.
